Wind farm bid next to Galloway reservoir submitted

A planning bid has been submitted for a new wind farm beside the Penwhirn Reservoir in Galloway, in south-west Scotland. The proposed development, called the Mark Hill project, would sit next to the reservoir, which supplies water to nearby communities. According to reports, the scheme would include multiple wind turbines and is now entering the planning process where environmental impacts, local concerns, and energy benefits will be assessed.

The proposal is part of Scotland’s wider push to expand renewable energy generation and meet net-zero targets through more onshore wind developments. Supporters argue projects like this help reduce reliance on fossil fuels and improve energy security, while critics often raise concerns about visual impact, wildlife, and effects on the local landscape and water environment.
